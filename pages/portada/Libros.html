My favourite book

Description

Name: Cien años de soledad

Author: Gabriel García Márquez

Sinopsis: It tells the story of a family

Why is it my favourite book?

Although my description does not seem to be quite interesting, "Cien años de soledad" is from a genre called Magic realism. It is able to mix magic and reality with an incredible structure. I also liked it because I could see the great influence this book has had on spanish authors from the late XX century.
